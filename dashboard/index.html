🍃 AQI Micro-Tracker Project

The AQI Micro-Tracker Dashboard is an advanced environmental monitoring application designed to provide citizens, researchers, and urban planners with real-time and historical air quality metrics across major Indian metropolitan areas.

Using satellite-derived reanalysis data and ground station measurements from the Open-Meteo API, the dashboard tracks key airborne pollutants including PM2.5, PM10, Carbon Monoxide (CO), Nitrogen Dioxide (NO₂), and Ozone.

📊 Air Quality Index (AQI) Reference Scale

AQI Range Category Color Code Health Implications
0 - 50 Good Green (#22c55e) Minimal health impact. Clean air.
51 - 100 Moderate Yellow (#eab308) Acceptable air quality; minor concern for sensitive individuals.
101 - 150 Poor Orange (#f97316) Breathing discomfort to sensitive people and children.
151 - 200 Very Poor Red (#ef4444) Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure to most individuals.
201+ Extremely Poor Purple (#a855f7) Severe respiratory impact; health warning for all residents.
